Designed the course after noticing how many confident answers in office
hours turned out to be someone repeating an explanation rather than one of
their own. Chairs the weekly review sessions and sets each week's instrument
before Idris runs it. Office hours by email; there is no fixed drop-in slot,
since the course's whole premise is that "I understand this" is a claim worth
checking rather than taking on trust.
