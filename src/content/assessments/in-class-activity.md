## The brief

> Show up to the instrument, not just the room.

Every session runs a short activity — a reconstruction, a review, a designed
test — and this component tracks whether you engaged with it as it happened,
not whether you can produce a polished version afterward. A rough, honest
attempt discussed in the room counts for more here than a clean answer
submitted without having done the week's exercise live.

## What you submit

Nothing separate. This mark is formed from your participation in each
week's in-class activity, recorded by the tutor running that session.
