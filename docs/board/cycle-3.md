## Moderator's conclusion — Round 3

**On the object-level question, the three seats converged, and this time I've checked it rather than taken it on report — it holds.** All three ran independent reasoning to the same verdict: add `pages/sources`, don't add `instruments`. Student reached it from occasion (Round 1's sentence needed the semester in it; this proposal doesn't); committee reached it from precedent (no institutional precedent for a refusal-bibliography, strong precedent for instrument banks, which makes the latter unsurprising and the former the one genuinely new thing) and from a structural fault on `LAD-4` that neither other seat named; designer reached it last, by finally running its own standing tests (`CON-5`) against its own proposal and failing on contact — a set of finished instruments is a maximally consumable artefact, in a course whose thesis is that consumption produces familiarity. Three routes, one destination, and — unlike Round 2's "convergence," which turned out to paper over an unanswered question — no seat here is asserting agreement it doesn't hold or leaving a live charge unaddressed. This is real.

**What the convergence does not resolve, and what I think the chair is actually choosing between.** Rejecting `instruments` answers this round's question narrowly — one segment, not two — but designer names, on the record and without being asked, three items the rejected proposal was trying to fix that are now exactly as unresolved as before Round 3: `CON-4` (the battery-of-tests deliverable) still has no row anywhere in the site's IA; `IA-5` (the worked example, including the deliberately-bad-instrument-beside-a-good-one designer asks the chair not to lose) is still unbuilt; `IA-4` (the ladder page) is still homeless. Committee's structural fault against Proposal 1 supplies a criterion for `IA-4`'s eventual home (cheapest to tear down, not most integrated, because `LAD-4` requires week 12 to be able to attack it) but does not supply the home itself. So the actual choice in front of the chair is not designer-vs-committee — that's settled — it's whether adding `pages/sources` this round is the answer to "what's missing," full stop, or whether it answers the narrower segment question while leaving the harder one (where do `CON-4`/`IA-4`/`IA-5` live) exactly where three rounds of the board have now found it: named, argued over, and still not built.
